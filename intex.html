Common questions

Most things answered before you pick up the phone. For anything else, use the chat or contact form.

Are you Gas Safe registered?
Yes. All our engineers are Gas Safe registered (Reg. No. 123456). You can verify this at gassaferegister.co.uk. We carry ID and can show our card on arrival.
Do you cover emergency callouts?
Yes — 24/7, 365 days a year. For gas leaks, call the National Gas Emergency line (0800 111 999) first to isolate the supply, then call us immediately. We aim to be on-site within 60 minutes for genuine emergencies.
How much does a new boiler cost?
Supply and fit typically ranges from £1,500 to £3,000+ depending on boiler brand, output size, and the complexity of the installation. We provide fixed quotes before any work starts — no surprises.
How quickly can I get a landlord gas cert?
We can usually attend within 24–48 hours. Same-day certificates are available subject to engineer availability. The certificate (CP12) is emailed on the day of inspection.
Do you carry parts on the van?
Yes — our engineers carry common parts for most boiler brands including Viessmann, Vokèra, Biasi, and Worcester. Most repairs are completed in a single visit.
